How to play

Rules

After you start a round, the screen shows a tense wait (“high noon”). At a random moment, DRAW! appears. You must react within 0.3 seconds by clicking the button or pressing Space.

Reacting before DRAW appears counts as a foul — same as hitting Enter too early in the terminal version.

Timing

The pause before DRAW is random (about 2 to 5 seconds). Your reaction time is measured from the instant DRAW is shown until you act.
